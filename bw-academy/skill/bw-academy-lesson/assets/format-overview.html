Session Breakdown

The six-part lesson template used for every BW Academy session.
1

Hook — Session Intro

Open with a bold, front-page-magazine-style intro that grabs attention and frames the session. A strong headline, an eye-catching image and a one-line teaser that tells learners why today's topic matters.

2

Watch — Teacher Video

5–10 min

The core teaching moment. A short video from the teacher that walks through the main idea of the session clearly and personally.

3

Think of It This Way — Quick Analogy

A short, memorable analogy that connects the new concept to something familiar from everyday life, so the idea sticks.

4

Explore — Go Deeper

5–10 min

A supporting resource that expands on the teaching: a YouTube video, or an article / PDF. Gives learners a second voice and more depth on the topic.

5

Check In — 3 Quick Questions

Three short questions to confirm understanding, each in a different format:

  • Pick One Tap to answer (multiple choice)
  • Show It Write your answer
  • Say It Record a 30-second audio response
6

Today's Challenge

Close with one practical action step learners take beyond the session, putting what they learned into practice in their real life this week.
